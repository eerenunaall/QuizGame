# Sound and show motion

Status: implemented (web). Code: `apps/web/src/lib/sound/*`, `apps/web/src/lib/audio.ts`,
`apps/web/src/lib/haptics.ts`, `apps/web/src/ui/anim/*`, `apps/web/src/styles/motion.css`.
Tools: `scripts/audio/render.ts` (`pnpm audio:render`). Tests: `lib/sound/sound.test.ts`,
`lib/audio.test.ts`, `ui/anim/anim.test.tsx`, `tests/e2e/show.spec.ts`.

## 1. Where the sound comes from (licence statement)

**Every sound is original and generated.** Nothing is downloaded and there are no audio files in the
repository. The cues are synthesised in the browser at run time (Web Audio oscillators, filtered
noise and a room impulse generated from a fixed seed) from the recipes in
`apps/web/src/lib/sound/palette.ts`. There is nothing third-party to license or attribute; the credits
page and `apps/web/src/assets/NOTICE.md` say "original, generated by scripts". Added size: about 35 KB of
source (about 9 KB gzipped), no binary assets. `scripts/audio/render.ts` renders the very same code offline in
headless Chromium to measure and calibrate levels (and to write WAVs for listening); it adds no
dependency beyond the Playwright already used by the e2e tests.

Autoplay: sound starts only after a user gesture (`sfx.unlock()`, unchanged: the TV's "press a key"
hint, a phone tap). Mute and the volume slider are honoured (`sfx.configure`). Reduced motion does not
mute anything (sound is not motion) but every animation below has a reduced-motion path.

## 2. One key, one timbre family

- **Key.** C major. Melodies use its pentatonic subset (C D E G A, also A minor pentatonic) so any
  cues that overlap (a phone and the TV answering together) are always consonant. The lobby ambience
  walks C - Am - F - G. A unit test checks that every pitched voice and glide target of every cue is a
  note of C major.
- **Voices.** `bell` (glassy glockenspiel: fundamental + octave + 3rd partial + shimmer), `mallet`
  (marimba / woodblock), `pad` (warm detuned saw, fanfares and ambience), `thump` (round kick / "bonk" with
  a mid "knock" so phone speakers can play it), `noise` (whooshes, risers, crashes, the drum roll), `click`
  (15 ms transient that lets a cue cut through a noisy room).
- **Loud room.** Bells carry their energy at 1-4 kHz where speech noise leaves room; sub-bass is kept
  modest (a TV speaker and a phone cannot play it anyway); every cue has a click or mid transient.

## 3. Mix

Chain: voices -> input -> compressor (threshold -4 dB, ratio 12, knee 6, a gentle limiter) -> soft clipper
-> master gain. A small generated room reverb is mixed in per cue.

- **Levels.** `lib/sound/mix.ts` is the intent (three tiers: UI -11 dB, MID -7 dB, MOMENT -3 dB peak at
  master 1.0); `pnpm audio:render --calibrate` measures each cue offline and writes the per-cue trim to
  `lib/sound/trim.generated.ts`; `pnpm audio:render --check` fails when a cue clips or drifts more than
  1.5 dB from its tier. Sequences (reveal, podium) use the trimmed cues, so a cue inside a longer score
  is as loud as on its own.
- **TV and phone ceilings.** `levelFor(volume, profile)`: gain = ceiling x volume^1.6 (a perceptual
  taper, so the lower half of the slider is usable). TV ceiling 1.0, phone ceiling 0.5 (-6 dB): a phone is
  held close to the ear and several phones share a room. `sfx.configure({ profile })` is set by
  `TvApp` ('tv') and `PlayApp` ('phone').
- **Weak TVs.** `data-motion="low"` (device gate) builds the lighter graph: no reverb, thinned drum rolls.
- **Phones do not tick.** The TV plays the countdown; a phone plays only personal feedback (tap, lock-in,
  its own correct / wrong held to the reveal's hit, combo, hit cues) and vibrates.

## 4. The cues

Keys marked (new) are additive; the original twenty keep working and sound better. `sfx.play(key,
{ streak, step, order, place, length, delayMs })` takes optional parameters; `sfx.playReveal`,
`sfx.playPodium`, `sfx.countdownTick(secondsLeft)`, `sfx.startAmbience()` / `stopAmbience()` are the
scored sequences.

| Key | Sounds like | Triggered |
|---|---|---|
| `tap` | woodblock tick | phone and TV UI taps, power / risk sheets |
| `join` | two bells, a fifth apart; each arrival climbs one rung of the scale | TV lobby on a new player; the phone's own join |
| `ready` (new) | marimba + bell, bright "pop" up | TV lobby when a player taps ready |
| `tick` / `tickUrgent` | mallet + click, pitch rises one rung per second over the last 5 s; the last 3 s are accented | TV answering, once per second via `countdownTick` |
| `countdown` / `go` | bell + kick on 3, 2, 1 (E, G, A); a full C chord + riser + kick on GO | TV 3-2-1-GO |
| `whoosh` | filtered noise sweep + soft thump | TV intermission between questions |
| `stinger` (new) | short riser + two-note rise | TV risk and sabotage pick screens |
| `roundStart` (new) | riser, kick, C-G-C bell chord | TV round intro |
| `question` | two rising mallets + bell | TV question appears |
| `lock` | click + kick + mallet (the "lock-in click") | phone answer lock-in; TV answers locked |
| `drumroll` (new) | snare-ish roll that accelerates and crescendos | start of the reveal sequence |
| `dim` (new) | soft falling mallet + click, lower for each wrong option | each wrong option dimming, in step with the picture |
| `revealHit` (new) | big kick + bell chord + crash | the right card lights up |
| `reveal` | roll into hit (the old single-shot reveal) | kept for compatibility |
| `correct` | rising bell arpeggio C-E-G-C, one rung more per streak step (up to two octaves), shimmer and pad from streak 3 | TV reveal; phone reveal at the hit, with the player's streak |
| `wrong` | soft bonk: low round thud + a falling minor third, nothing harsh | TV reveal when nobody was right; phone when wrong |
| `combo` (new) | fire-whoosh and two rising bells, higher with the streak | phone reveal with a streak of 2+ |
| `score` / `rankUp` | sparkle run / quick glide + bell | TV score screen / when a player climbs |
| `final` | drum roll + riser into a low pad stab and bell chord | TV final-round splash |
| `fanfare` / `podium` (new) | brass-like chord stabs + bells; podium hits for 3rd, 2nd, then the winner | TV results (`playPodium`, timed with the podium animation) |
| `rematch` (new) | five-bell run up + riser | TV rematch button |
| `power` | swell + shimmer | TV power resolution for Cover / Duel / Pot / stake |
| `fifty` (new) | scissor snip + two falling plucks | 50/50 |
| `doubleDown` (new) | two hits, then an octave jump | Double Down |
| `shield` | glassy bell pair + shimmer | blocked sabotage (TV and phone) |
| `hitJam` `hitShuffle` `hitFog` `hitLockout` `hitTax` `hitCap` `hitCut` `hitBlind` (new) | one gesture per sabotage kind (clock stutter, scrambled run, dark pad + puff, padlock clank, falling coins, double thud, slice, lights-out), all mallet + thump + noise | TV power resolution (`hitSoundFor(effect)`); the target's phone (`HitImpact`) |
| `hit`, `sabotage` | generic hit / falling minor-third wobble | kept; no longer triggered by the screens |
| ambience (new) | very quiet C - Am - F - G pads with an occasional music-box note, 66 bpm | TV lobby only; fades out when the game starts; off when muted |

Measured levels of every cue (peak / rms / 0.5-4 kHz band, dBFS) come from `pnpm audio:render`; run it
after any change to `palette.ts`, `engine.ts` or `mix.ts`, then `--calibrate` and commit
`trim.generated.ts`. `pnpm audio:render -- --wav <dir>` writes a WAV per cue and an `index.html`
soundboard to listen to the palette.

## 5. Show motion

One orchestrated moment per phase, nothing scattered. All timings that picture and sound share live in
`lib/sound/timeline.ts` (`revealPlan`, `tickPlan`, `PODIUM_PLAN`).

| Phase | The moment | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Any (TV) | a soft band of light sweeps the stage when a phase begins (also the page-load entrance) | `ui/anim/PhaseSweep` in `TvFrame` |
| Any (phone) | the screen rises 14 px and fades in; state is kept (no remount) | `ui/anim/usePhaseEntrance` in `PhoneFrame` |
| Lobby | a joining avatar springs in with a halo of its ring colour; the join cue climbs the scale | `Avatar arrive`, `useTvSounds` |
| Answering | the clock ring pulses outward in the last 3 s, a red edge vignette beats once per second, the TV ticks speed up (1 -> 2 -> 4 per second) and rise in pitch; phones get the vignette and a vibration beat per second (3 light, 2, 1 firm) | `CountdownRing`, `UrgencyVignette`, `haptics.ts` |
| Lock-in | the card stamps down, a white flash crosses it, click + buzz | `AnswerCard` `.locked`, `haptic('lock')` |
| Reveal | drum roll; the wrong options dim one by one (falling ticks); the right card pops, a green frame and a sparkle burst land on the hit, then the faces and the right / wrong badges follow; the phone holds its verdict (three roll dots) until the same hit | `revealPlan`, `TvQuestion`, `AnswerCard`, `RevealBurst`, `RollDots` |
| Streaks | a flame with the count on the TV strip and the phone header from 2 in a row; a combo toast on the phone (2+) and a combo corner on the TV (3+); the correct arpeggio grows with the streak | `StreakFlame`, `ComboToast` |
| Score | each row has a bar in the player's colour that grows from the old share to the new one with a slight overshoot; rows that climbed get a gold pulse and a chevron | `ScoreBar`, `.qp-climb` |
| Power / hit | each lifeline and sabotage kind has its own cue; on the target's phone a coloured edge flash, a page shake and a vibration (a blocked hit gets a shield glint and no shake) | `TvPower`, `HitImpact` |
| Results | third place lands, then second, a drum roll, then the winner under a spotlight with the cannons; each score counts up when its step lands; confetti follows the theme (party / venue-gold / high-contrast / gusto) | `PODIUM_PLAN`, `TvResults`, `lib/confetti` |

Rules kept: transform and opacity only (the score bar, the vignette and the burst are static layers whose
transform or opacity moves); one `requestAnimationFrame` loop per timer; no flex `gap`, `inset` or
`aspect-ratio` (2020 smart-TV Chromium); TV text stays inside the 5% safe area (`tests/e2e/show.spec.ts`
measures it while everything moves).

**Reduced motion / low power.** `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses every animation to its end state
(global.css) but keeps delays, so the reveal still lands in order, just without movement; `data-motion="low"`
(weak TVs) removes the decorative layers outright (`.qp-deco`) and builds the lighter sound graph. Under
reduced motion the page shake, the flash, the vibration pulses (urgency, hits, combo) and the confetti are
dropped; taps, lock-in and wrong-answer vibration stay.

**Performance.** `tests/e2e/show.spec.ts` replays a reveal and a score screen at 4x CPU throttling and checks
the frame gaps (p95 under 120 ms in headless software rendering).
